About Me

I am a postdoc researcher at the University of Sydney. My host is Prof. Qiang Tang. My research focuses on applied cryptography, with their applications in blockchains. I obtained the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering department at HKUST. I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Dimitris Papadopoulos during my PhD.
